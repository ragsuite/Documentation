**Edition:** Community

Crawl brings public or allowed website content into your project so AI Search and AI Chatbot can cite it.

Product positioning on [ragsuite.de](https://www.ragsuite.de) describes crawl with configurable depth (**1–5**) alongside upload of PDF, DOCX, and TXT.

## Guidance

- Prefer authoritative sources your organization owns or is licensed to use.
- Re-crawl when source sites change materially.
- Verify citations after large crawl jobs before publishing widgets widely.
