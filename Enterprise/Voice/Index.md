**Edition:** Enterprise · **Accuracy:** Shipped (entitlement-gated)

**Voice** adds browser speech-to-text and text-to-speech controls (mic / speaker) on **AI Chatbot** and **AI Search** widgets. Entitlements such as `voice` / `voice:use` gate the feature.

This is **not** [AI Voice Pilot](/Enterprise/AIVoicePilot/Index) (dashboard speak-and-hear RAG with ElevenLabs). Keep the two Enterprise modules separate when configuring projects.

## Important

Offline keys issued **before** the voice module was added may **not** include voice until your vendor re-issues the key with the full Enterprise entitlement set. Until then, widgets look like Community (no mic/speaker).

Community Edition does not include voice icons/controls on widgets.
