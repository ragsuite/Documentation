**Edition:** Enterprise · **Accuracy:** Shipped

**AI Voice Pilot** is an Enterprise module for **spoken** Q&A against your project knowledge base: you speak, the system retrieves grounded answers, and replies can be spoken aloud (provider setup uses **ElevenLabs** — API key plus a selected voice).

It is **not** [Voice](/Enterprise/Voice/Index) (browser STT/TTS mic and speaker controls on AI Chatbot and AI Search widgets). It is **not** [Admin Assistant](/Console/AdminAssistant/Index) or [AI Chatbot](/Console/AIChatbot/Index).

## What you get

- Dashboard **AI Voice Pilot** workspace (Pilot, Voices, and Settings).
- Voice↔voice RAG against the active project’s sources.
- Optional Voice Pilot surface on chatbot **Layout 2** (Home CTA and footer tab) when Enterprise entitlements allow it.

Community Edition may show a **locked teaser**; full Voice Pilot requires an Enterprise license and the AI Voice Pilot module in your entitled bundle.

## Permissions

With Enterprise organization RBAC, project access can include Voice Pilot **use** and **settings** permissions. Operators without those permissions cannot configure or run Pilot for that project.
