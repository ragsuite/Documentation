**Edition:** Community (`llm_providers`)

RAGSuite is **model-agnostic**. Categories highlighted on [ragsuite.de](https://www.ragsuite.de):

## Frontier APIs

Bring your own key — switch per project. Examples called out on the marketing site:

- OpenAI
- Anthropic Claude
- Google Gemini
- Azure OpenAI

## EU-sovereign

Processing stays under EU jurisdiction when you choose these providers (per their terms):

- Mistral
- Aleph Alpha
- IONOS AI
- OVHcloud AI

## Self-hosted / compatible

- **Ollama** (Llama, Mistral, Qwen, and other local models)
- **vLLM** and other **OpenAI-compatible** endpoints

You can point RAGSuite at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint your security team approves.

## Enterprise quality loop

[Compare Models](/Enterprise/CompareModels/Index) runs one query across models side by side and scores answers — Enterprise feature on your infrastructure.
