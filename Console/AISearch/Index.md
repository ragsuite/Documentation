**Edition:** Community

AI Search finds answers across the material you have ingested — manuals, policies, PDFs, crawled pages, and connected sources — and returns results that link back to their sources.

## In the console

Use Search mode in the admin console to ask natural-language questions against your project knowledge. Results are grounded in your corpus; treat citations as the proof path before acting on an answer.

## Embeddable widget

Publish search where your team already works (website, intranet, apps) via the search widget embed paths. See [Widgets & embeds](/SourcesAndConnectors/Widgets/Index).
